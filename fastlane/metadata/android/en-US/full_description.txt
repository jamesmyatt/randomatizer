Minimalist, flat dice roller for board games.

<ul>
<li>2 modes:
<ul>
<li><b>Basic</b>: 1–12 six-sided dice, shown as pips.</li>
<li><b>Advanced</b>: up to 12 each of d4, d6, d8, d10, d12, d20 and d100, shown as numbers.</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Results and optional total.</li>
<li>Collapsible session history, never saved.</li>
<li>System colors. Light, dark or system theme. Custom dice colors, with pips that always contrast.</li>
</ul>

<b>Private, open and fair</b>: no internet permission, no ads, no tracking, no Google Play Services. Rolls use a secure random number generator.
