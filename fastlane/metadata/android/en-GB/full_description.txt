Minimalist, flat dice roller for board games.

<ul>
<li><b>Basic</b>: 1–10 six-sided dice, shown as pips.</li>
<li><b>Advanced</b>: up to 10 each of d4, d6, d8, d10, d12, d20 and d100, shown as numbers.</li>
<li>Results and optional total.</li>
<li>Session history, never saved.</li>
<li>System colours and light/dark mode. Dice colours: system, inverted or custom, with contrast checks.</li>
</ul>

<b>Private, open and fair</b>: no internet permission, no ads, no tracking, no Google Play Services. Rolls use a secure random number generator.
